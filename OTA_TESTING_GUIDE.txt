CHORD COMPANION APP — FIRMWARE OTA UPDATE GUIDE FOR TESTERS
============================================================

1. OVERVIEW
-----------
The CHORD Companion App performs Over-The-Air (OTA) firmware updates over
Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) using Firebase Storage for firmware distribution,
Nordic SMP (mcumgr) for transfer, and MCUboot dual-slot image swapping.


2. AUTOMATIC HARDWARE SELECTION (DEV KIT VS. PRODUCTION)
--------------------------------------------------------
You do NOT need different app builds for Dev Kit vs. Production hardware.
When the app connects to a CHORD unit, it reads the device's Hardware Revision
over BLE (Device Information Service, characteristic 0x2A27):

  - Dev Kit (0x2A27 = "nrf5340dk"):
      Checks: firmware/manifests/nrf5340dk/
      Images: firmware/images/nrf5340dk/

  - Production Body Unit (0x2A27 = "chord_body_c51ce1a"):
      Checks: firmware/manifests/chord_body_c51ce1a/
      Images: firmware/images/chord_body_c51ce1a/

You can verify which hardware string the connected unit reports under:
  Profile & Settings -> Device Diagnostics -> Hardware Revision


3. PRE-CONDITIONS (ENFORCED BY THE APP)
---------------------------------------
Before an OTA upload can start, the app checks:
  1) Device is connected over BLE.
  2) Battery level is at least 30% (if known).
  3) Audio is NOT currently streaming (pause music/calls before updating).


4. HOW TO TEST CLOUD OTA (FIREBASE STORAGE)
-------------------------------------------
1. Sign in to the app and connect to your CHORD device.
2. Open Profile & Settings -> CHORD Management & Actions -> Check Firmware Update.
3. The app automatically queries Firebase Storage for manifests matching the
   device's Hardware Revision (0x2A27) and compares them against the running
   firmware version (characteristic 9e32000e).
4. If a newer version is found (for example, v0.3.0 -> v0.3.2):
   - The sheet displays the target version, board, binary size, and release notes.
   - Tap "Download & Install vX.Y.Z".
5. The app downloads the .signed.bin file, verifies its byte size, SHA-256 hash,
   and 32-byte MCUboot header, and automatically begins the BLE SMP upload.


5. WHAT HAPPENS DURING THE UPDATE (DO NOT POWER OFF)
----------------------------------------------------
1. Upload (SMP Group 1, ID 1):
   - Firmware chunks are sent over BLE SMP with a live progress bar (0-100%).
2. Test-Only Mode (SMP Group 1, ID 0, confirm: false):
   - The app marks Slot 1 as "Test-Only" and NEVER confirms from the app.
   - The CHORD firmware is responsible for confirming itself after booting.
3. Reboot & MCUboot Swap (up to 90 seconds):
   - The unit reboots and BLE disconnects while MCUboot swaps Slot 1 -> Slot 0.
   - On nRF5340, this swap typically takes 45-60 seconds.
   - Keep the unit powered on and near the phone.
4. Auto-Reconnect & Result:
   - The app automatically reconnects once the unit finishes swapping and
     reads the running firmware version (9e32000e):
       * Green "Firmware Update Succeeded": running version matches target.
       * Amber "Firmware Rolled Back by MCUboot": new firmware failed to boot/
         confirm and MCUboot reverted to the previous working version.


6. TROUBLESHOOTING
------------------
- "Cloud update check failed" / permission error:
  Ensure you are signed into the app and Firebase Storage Rules allow
  authenticated reads on /firmware/{allPaths=**}.
- "Hardware mismatch" error:
  The manifest or binary does not match the connected unit's Hardware Revision
  (0x2A27), or targets the network core instead of the application core.
- Swap countdown reaches 90s without reconnecting:
  Check the unit's status LED. If off, press the power button to wake it, then
  reconnect from the app and check Device Diagnostics -> Firmware Version &
  Active Fault Code.
